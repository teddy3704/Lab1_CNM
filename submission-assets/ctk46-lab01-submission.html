1. Mo ta bai tap

Project duoc xay dung bang Next.js 16 theo App Router va duoc chinh sua thanh mot landing page ca nhan theo huong editorial critique board. Muc tieu cua bai lam khong chi la hien thong tin sinh vien, ma con chung minh kha nang to chuc bo cuc, xay dung he thong giao dien va trien khai mot luong fullstack nho nhung ro rang de phuc vu viec demo va thuyet trinh.

Trang chu hien tai da bao gom hero ca nhan, architecture section, workflow, submission readiness va mot khoi Project Brief dung Server Actions + zod de validate du lieu dau vao va sinh ra creative blueprint typed tra ve giao dien.
